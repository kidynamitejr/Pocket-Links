PocketLinks.

Nine holes at Hollow Creek, par 36. Read the wind, pick your club, and swing.

  1. Tap the map to aim. The club picks itself for the distance, or change it with the arrows.
  2. On the swing pad, pull down to set power, then swipe straight back up to hit.
  3. Swipe up to the right and the ball slices right. Swipe up to the left and it hooks left.

    On iPhone, open this in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to play it like an app.
